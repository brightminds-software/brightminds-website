What Does “Hosting a Website” Mean?

Hosting means placing your website files on a server so people can access your site using the internet.

Without hosting, your website cannot be seen by anyone online.

Why We Recommend Truehost Kenya

  • Pay using M-Pesa
  • Kenyan support team
  • Beginner-friendly dashboard
  • Free SSL & fast servers

Step 1: Visit Truehost Kenya Website

Open your browser and go to the official Truehost Kenya website.

Tip: Always confirm you are on the correct site before making payments.

Step 2: Choose a Hosting Plan

For beginners, shared hosting is more than enough.

  • Personal websites
  • Blogs
  • Small business websites

Step 3: Register or Use an Existing Domain

You can buy a new domain or connect one you already own.

Example: yourwebsite.co.ke

Step 4: Pay Using M-Pesa

At checkout, select M-Pesa as your payment method.

Enter your phone number and confirm the STK Push on your phone.

Step 5: Access Your Hosting Dashboard

After payment, Truehost will send login details to your email.

From the dashboard, you can:

  • Upload website files
  • Install WordPress
  • Manage domains & emails

Step 6: Upload Your Website

If you built your site using HTML/CSS:

  • Open File Manager
  • Upload files to public_html
  • Visit your domain
